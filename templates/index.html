Deep Neural Architecture

Microscopic vision.
Sub-second precision.

A purpose-built multi-stage convolutional neural network trained to identify complex plant cellular degradation before crop loss occurs.

Stage 01

256×256 Spectral Ingestion

Raw leaf photographs are normalized across 3 spectral channels (RGB) and conditioned against varied lighting, tilt angles, and focal distortions via real-time geometric augmentation.

Stage 02

Convolutional Pyramids

5 sequential Conv2D stages isolate micro-lesions, necrotic halos, fungal sporulation textures, and chlorotic veining patterns across 128 multi-scale feature filters.

Stage 03

38-Class Classification

58.1 million deep parameters terminate into a dense 1024-node hidden layer with dropout regularization, delivering calibrated Softmax probability distributions across 38 cultivars.
